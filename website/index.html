Weak spots of paper and shared copies

Why Traditional Verification Fails

Copies Everywhere

Over-sharing

A full photocopy is handed over even when only a name or an age is needed, and nobody can see where it ends up.

Easy to Forge

Document tampering

Paper and scanned copies are simple to edit, and a verifier has no quick way to tell a genuine one from a forgery.

Single Points of Failure

Data leaks

Plain-text records in one database are an attractive target, and a single leak exposes everything at once.

What happens when you submit

The 7-Step BID Pipeline

01

Step 01

Data Entry

You enter your details. The website checks the format of each field.

02

Step 02

Secure Request

The details are sent as JSON to the blockchain server's /add_block route.

03

Step 03

Encryption

The server encrypts the data with AES (Fernet) before it is stored.

04

Step 04

SHA-256 Hash

A 64-character SHA-256 hash is computed over the block and the previous hash.

05

Step 05

Chain Link

The new block points to the previous one, so editing any block breaks the chain.

06

Step 06

BID Issued

The block's hash becomes your unique BID, returned to you on this page.

07

Step 07

You Choose What to Share

In the BID app, switch on only the fields you want. Only those are fetched and sent phone to phone.

Key Advantages

Encrypted Storage

Personal data is stored encrypted, not as plain text.

Tamper-Evident

Each block is hash-linked, and the chain can be validated at any time.

Selective Sharing

Share a name without sharing an Aadhaar number. You decide per field.

Works Offline

Phone-to-phone sharing needs no internet connection.

One BID, No Photocopies

Register once and reuse the same BID everywhere.

Simple to Run

A small Flask server and a Flutter app. Easy to demo and extend.
